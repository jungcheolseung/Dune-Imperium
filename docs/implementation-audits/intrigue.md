# Intrigue implementation audit

This checklist records the Intrigue play boundary and every transcribed
Intrigue card. `docs/rules/player-turns.md` and
`docs/rules/combat-and-round-end.md` remain the rules sources of truth; Dune
Cards Hub card images are the printed-text reference and the DIU dataset is a
transcription bootstrap only.

## Effect DSL

Intrigue cards are transcribed with the composable DSL in
`content/uprising/effect_dsl.py` and executed by
`rules/effect_interpreter.py`.

| Piece | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `IntrigueOption(timing, sections)` | One way to play the card. `A —OR— B` cards have two options; stacked lines share one option. |
| `EffectSection(rewards, condition, cost)` | One printed line. It is *applicable* when its condition holds (or has none). |
| Conditions | `InfluenceAtLeast`, `HasHighCouncil`, `SpiesPlacedAtLeast`, `CompletedContractsAtLeast`, `SandwormsInConflictAtLeast`, `GainedSpiceThisTurn`, `SpiceMustFlowCardsAtLeast` (deck, hand, discard and play copies; a trashed copy has left the game), `OpponentAllianceInfluenceAtLeast` (a track with the printed Influence whose Alliance an opponent holds). Conditions read the full game state. |
| Triggers | `IntrigueOption.trigger` marks an option that does nothing when played: the card waits face up and its sections fire later [FAQ p. 2]. `OnRevealAcquisitionThisRound` fires once per card the owner acquires during their own Reveal turn; `OnTroopsLostAtConflictEnd` is Harvest Cells', which since 2026-10-06 never waits face up: it is played only in the post-reward Conflict-end window once its loss holds (`IntriguePlayBlock.CONFLICT_END`, user ruling, OQ-016). Trigger sections must be free and unconditional. ("When you deploy three or more units to the Conflict in a single turn" stopped being a trigger on 2026-10-03: it is the play condition `UnitsDeployedThisTurnAtLeast`, OQ-016.) |
| Costs | `PayResources` (automatic), `LoseInfluence`, `DiscardFromHand`, `RecallSpy`, `RetreatTroops`, `FlipBattleCard` (choice slots; the flip targets the owner's face-up won Conflict cards and Objectives bearing the printed icon or the wild icon — an Objective "counts as a Conflict card you've already won" [Objective card], OQ-005 2026-10-04) |
| Rewards | `GainResources`, `GainVictoryPoints`, `RecruitTroops`, `DrawPersonalCards`, `DrawIntrigueCards`, `GainInfluence` (a choice slot unless one Faction is allowed), `DestroyShieldWall` (a detonate/keep choice slot while the token is present), `DeployFromGarrison` (a count choice slot), `SummonSandworm` (automatic; no effect against a protected Conflict), `TrashPersonalCard` (an optional trash slot over hand, discard and play), `PlaceSpy` (a placement slot, recall-first when supply is empty), `RetreatTroops` (a count slot, also usable as a cost), `TakeContract` (automatic; opens the Contract market frame), `GainCombatStrength` (automatic; adds to `combat_strength` during Combat), `AcquireCardUpTo` (a target choice slot over the Imperium Row and Reserve within a printed cost cap), `SetAsideImperiumRowCard` (a Row choice slot; the removed card waits in the owner's public set-aside zone) |

An option is playable when at least one section applies, the summed resource
costs are affordable, the player has enough total Influence for every
`LoseInfluence` step, and enough hand cards for every `DiscardFromHand` step.
A line with a cost (left of an arrow: resources, a discard or trash, an
Intrigue card, a troop loss, a Spy recall, ...) is also played only when one
of its own rewards can change something now (OQ-113 (C), user ruling
2026-10-24 "비용을 낸 것만 보장", codec v154; on top of "To play an Intrigue
card, you must meet its conditions and pay its costs." [FAQ p. 2] and OQ-071's
"비용만 내고 보상을 받지 못하는 경우는 없어야 한다"). A line without a cost is
played even when every reward fizzles: the Intrigue card itself is no cost,
as a card box that fizzles still lets its Imperium card be played. Printed
conditions still block (`EffectSection.condition`: a High Council seat, an
Influence level, a Contract or Spy count, ...), and so does Coercive
Negotiation's three Contracts in the bank (OQ-064, not asked again). History:
from 2026-10-06 (user ruling "원로회 자리 차지 안 하고 아무 효과 없이 책략을 쓸
수 없는거지"; OQ-071 card extension 2) to codec v153 every option needed at
least one reward that could change something. The check is
`effect_interpreter._no_effect_block`, which runs
`reward_can_change_something` per reward class over each line with a cost,
read after the option's resource cost and counting a cost that feeds a reward
of its own line (a discarded card reshuffled and drawn, a trashed non-Twisted
Intrigue shuffled back, a troop lost to pay made a specimen, a lowered
Faction raised again). A recruit counts only with a troop in the supply or,
under Immortality, a specimen to return, in or out of a turn
(`_troop_can_join`; a recorded OQ-030 shortfall does not count). One live
reward is enough: the rest fizzle once played. Only a Contract with CHOAM off
still blocks whatever else the option offers, because it cannot be resolved
at all (`_choice_reward_block`). A choice with nothing to act on in a line
without a cost is played and confirmed with one row: `skip_intrigue_top_card`
(Twisted Controlled, empty deck), `skip_intrigue_set_aside` (Manipulate, empty
Imperium Row) and `skip_intrigue_trash` (Twisted Devious' mandatory trash,
empty hand); False Orders before this turn's Agent is sent fizzles (no Spy
frame opens). A play whose lines all lack a cost and all fizzle logs the
public event `intrigue_without_effect`
(`effect_interpreter.play_changes_nothing`); the play server warns a human
seat before the click ("지금 내면 아무 효과가 없음", shortfall kind
`no_effect`), and such a play can be undone
(`test_an_intrigue_play_without_effect_is_warned_and_can_be_taken_back`). A
greyed row for a blocked cost line names every dead reward
(`display.unavailable`, joined with "; " / " · "). Navigation options use the
same check (OQ-039, project convention; a Navigation card's cost lines stay
blocked). Codec v154.
Playing the card pays the resource costs immediately. If the applicable
sections contain choice slots, an `intrigue_choice` frame resolves them one
action at a time (all cost slots first, then reward slots); each step applies
its effect at once so later steps see the updated state. Once the cost slots
are paid the owner may resolve the automatic rewards at any point among the
reward slots (`resolve_intrigue_rewards`, OQ-015 (d)); otherwise they resolve
after the last slot. Draws come last within the automatic rewards so a
reshuffle chance frame sits on top of the stack, and only then is the card
discarded. Cards printing separate lines without `—OR—` follow OQ-058 instead
(see "Mandatory costs" below).

## Play boundary

| Area | Implemented behavior | Rule-sensitive note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timing | Plot options are offered while the owner holds the `turn`, `agent_effects`, or `reveal` frame during Player Turns (`PLOT_FRAME_KINDS`). | The moment the turn choice is offered is treated as inside the owner's turn, so a Plot card may be played before committing to an Agent or Reveal turn (OQ-015 (a)). An Agent turn ends only at the owner's `finish_agent_turn` and a Reveal turn at `finish_reveal`, so a Plot may still be played after the last pending group resolves, until that press (OQ-095, user ruling 2026-09-30; until then the frame auto-advanced and the window closed with the last pending Agent-turn group). An "At the start of your turn" option (Withdrawn) is offered only on the `turn` frame as the turn's first action (OQ-095 (6), user ruling 2026-10-04; `IntriguePlayBlock.TURN_STARTED`). Project convention. |
| Endgame timing | Entering Endgame, each player clockwise from the First Player gets one `endgame_intrigue` window offering their Endgame Intrigue options, every wild battle icon pair they hold, and pass [Main pp. 7, 15, 20]. Plays and matches interleave freely; passing closes the window for good and the last pass finishes the game (OQ-001 convention). With no held Intrigue and no wild pair the game finishes without windows. | A flipped or matched card is face down at once, so later plays and matches in the same window see it spent. The window replaces the earlier single-unambiguous-pair auto path; ambiguous pairs are now ordinary choices. |
| Combat timing | Combat options are offered in the `combat_intrigue` frame to the participant who currently holds priority; playing one restarts the consecutive-pass count and its strength applies immediately [Main p. 14]. The player's next pass only hands priority on and is not counted (`played_at_priority`; since codec v139, 2026-10-07), so in the worked example Abby still decides after John passes again [Main p. 15]; a lone participant's pass after playing ends the step (project convention; [combat-and-round-end.md](../rules/combat-and-round-end.md) §2). Choice slots open on top of the Combat frame and return to it. | Only players with units in the Conflict ever hold the frame, so `GainCombatStrength` never lands on a unit-less player. No Uprising Intrigue card turned out to need "when you win" timing. |
| Retreats in Combat | `retreat_intrigue_troops(count)` moves troops to the garrison and removes two strength each; a player left without units keeps no strength [Main pp. 12, 14]. After the card finishes, `refresh_combat_participants` drops unit-less players from the loop at once and hands priority to the next remaining participant (OQ-003 convention). | If nobody remains the Intrigue step ends and rewards resolve. Players without units at Combat start never enter the loop. |
| Reveal-turn draws | (2026-09-01, OQ-015(c)) Plot options that draw personal cards — or acquire a card to hand (`AcquireCardUpTo.to_hand_if` holding) — are offered during the `reveal` frame too. A card that lands in hand is revealed on the spot [FAQ p. 3]: it moves hand → `in_play` and contributes its own Persuasion, sword, resources and choice effects to the same Reveal turn (`reveal_late_arrivals`, `grant_late_reveal_effects` in `rules/reveal_turn.py`). | Amounts already paid out earlier in the Reveal are final and are never recomputed or clawed back; a late arrival adds only the increment it causes for cards already revealed, and the eligibility for that increment is judged again at arrival time. The three cross-effects in the current content (Stilgar, Sardaukar Coordination, Leadership) carry no eligibility condition, so that re-check is always vacuous (since 2026-09-26 Stilgar counts Fremen cards in play and Leadership tops its count up after every step, OQ-057); `tests/unit/rules/test_reveal_turn.py` pins it so a future cross-effect *with* a condition fails against this claw-back-free implementation. |
| Face-up triggers | Playing a triggered option moves the card from the hidden Intrigue hand to the owner's public `intrigue_faceup` zone [FAQ p. 2]; no choice frame opens and no reward resolves yet. `rules/intrigue_triggers.py` fires the per-acquisition trigger at every Reveal-turn acquisition site (Persuasion Reserve/Row acquisitions and Intrigue-effect acquisitions made while the owner's Reveal frame is open) and recruits are supply-limited. When the owner's Reveal turn ends, the card moves to the Intrigue discard whether or not it ever fired (OQ-016). Since 2026-10-07 two non-trigger Plots whose effect waits for the owner's Reveal turn share the zone and its expiry, as [FAQ p. 2] says ("keep it face up in front of you until then, then use and discard it"): Tleilaxu Puppet's Plot and Manipulate. Their effect is recorded when played as before; only the card waits (`intrigue_triggers.waits_for_reveal_turn`, event `intrigue_kept_faceup`) and `expire_reveal_faceup_intrigue` discards it with Call to Arms when that Reveal turn ends (`intrigue_expired`), also when it was played during that Reveal turn. Before, both went straight to the Intrigue discard (the app still does). Seat panels show the face-up zone since the same day (e2e `scripts/e2e/intrigue_faceup.py`; before, it was never drawn). From 2026-10-06 to codec v153 a triggered option was played only if its firing could change something (`effect_interpreter._trigger_option_block`, OQ-016 addendum): Call to Arms needed a troop it could recruit, and in the owner's open Reveal a card still acquirable (`_reveal_acquisition_ahead`, `OptionBlock.NO_ACQUISITION_AHEAD`); Harvest Cells was filtered by its Conflict-end window (`combat._harvest_cells_has_effect`). Since codec v154 (OQ-113 (C), user ruling 2026-10-24) a triggered option has no cost and is played even when its firing could change nothing: Call to Arms with no troop to recruit or nothing left to acquire, and Harvest Cells in its Conflict-end window (`combat.conflict_end_trigger_block` checks only the loss and the play's own conditions; `_harvest_cells_has_effect` now only decides whether the play is noted as `intrigue_without_effect`, which the page warns of beforehand). Since 2026-10-09 that window itself opens on public facts, offering only the decline when no copy is playable (`combat._conflict_end_window_opens`, OQ-103). | Face-up identity is public in every observation. Agent-turn acquisitions (Price is No Object) are outside the printed window. The reshuffle never touches the face-up zone. |
| Spice-gained conditions | `GainedSpiceThisTurn` reads two `PlayerState` counters kept beside the deployment ones: `spice_at_turn_start` snapshots the owner's Spice as their turn opens and `spice_spent_turn` records what they spend during it (space costs, Spice-paying Agent effects, the three-Spice Reveal Influence payment, Intrigue costs), so gained = current − snapshot + spent, gross of spending. The FAQ requires a card's conditions to be met when it is played and reserves the face-up wait for effects that apply on a later Reveal turn [FAQ p. 2], so a backward-looking "If … this turn" card is offered only while its condition holds. | Since 2026-10-06 the Harvest Contract reads the same turn-wide counters: "gaining the amount of spice shown during that turn (in total, including from sources other than the space itself)" [Main p. 16], so Spice gained before the Agent placement (a Plot on the turn frame) counts too (`effects.eligible_agent_contract_ids`); until then it used a separate placement-scoped count. Combat-phase Spice movement is not recorded; the counters reset before they are ever read again. |
| Deployment condition | `PlayerState.units_deployed_turn` counts the units its owner moves into the Conflict during their own turn — Agent deployments, Intrigue deploys, Reveal-turn entries, Maker summons and sandworm rewards — because troops and sandworms are both units [Main p. 12] and a summoned sandworm is immediately deployed [Main p. 20]; an OQ-029 withdrawal or a retreat lowers it. `units_deployed_peak` keeps the turn's highest count (`combat_deployment.record_deployment_peak` after every applied transition; a withdrawal lowers it too, a retreat does not). Both reset when the owner's next turn opens; the Round Start Control defense deployment happens outside any turn and is not counted. `UnitsDeployedThisTurnAtLeast(count)` reads the peak: "You need to have a moment in time when there are 3 units in the conflict that were deployed to the conflict this turn, then that requirement becomes true." (Message from designer). | Since 2026-10-03 (codec v129, OQ-016) Distraction and Coercive Negotiation are Plots played once the condition holds [FAQ p. 2] [Board Guide p. 10], and resolve at once; until then they were face-up triggers offered after every transition (`offer_deployment_triggers`, `intrigue_trigger_spy`, `decline_intrigue_trigger`), a model the official text never described. Playing one commits the minimum (`units_deployed_committed`), so a withdrawal cannot drop below it (OQ-029). |
| Set-aside Row cards | `SetAsideImperiumRowCard` removes one Imperium Row card into the owner's public `imperium_set_aside` zone and refills the Row at once [Manipulate card]. During the owner's Reveal turn this round, `acquire_manipulated_imperium` acquires it for one Persuasion less (floored at zero) through the shared acquire-box, Contract-completion and Call to Arms plumbing; the Row is untouched. When that Reveal turn ends, a still-set-aside card moves to `imperium_removed` and has left the game [FAQ p. 3]. The Manipulate card itself waits face up until then (see Face-up triggers; 2026-10-07, `test_manipulate_stays_face_up_until_its_owners_reveal_turn_ends`). | Opponents are never offered the card. Since 2026-10-06 the FAQ's other means are implemented: "You may use other means to acquire the card (for example: Bypass Protocol, Boundless Ambition), though the 1 persuasion discount will not apply." [FAQ p. 3]. The owner's other acquire effects -- `AcquireCardUpTo` (Inspire Awe, Impress), Price is No Object, Tleilaxu Master and Princess Irulan's Chronicler's Insight -- also offer the owner's own set-aside card, checked at its printed cost against the effect's limit (the printed cap, or the Solari Price is No Object can pay; `acquisition.acquirable_imperium_instance_ids(..., player=)`); taking it leaves the set-aside zone without a Row refill (`acquisition.take_acquired_imperium_card`) and resolves as a normal acquisition. Reveal Persuasion buys it only through the discounted `acquire_manipulated_imperium`. Engineered Miracle's Command, "Trash this card → Acquire a card from the Imperium Row." [Engineered Miracle card], cannot reach it: the card is no longer in the Row. Tests: `tests/unit/rules/test_acquisition.py::test_price_is_no_object_buys_its_owners_set_aside_card_at_the_printed_cost`, `tests/unit/rules/test_intrigue.py` (`test_inspire_awe_reaches_its_owners_set_aside_card_at_the_printed_cost`, `test_inspire_awe_on_a_set_aside_card_is_a_full_acquisition`), `tests/unit/rules/test_immortality_imperium_choices.py::test_tleilaxu_master_reaches_its_owners_set_aside_card_at_the_printed_cost`, `tests/unit/rules/test_leader_abilities.py::test_chroniclers_insight_reaches_the_owners_set_aside_one_cost_card`. |
| Cost-capped acquisition | An `AcquireCardUpTo` slot offers every Imperium Row card and non-empty Reserve stack whose printed cost is within the cap [Main p. 13]; no Persuasion is spent and the acquisition is not declinable. The card lands in the owner's discard pile [Main pp. 6, 13], or in hand when the card text's condition holds at resolution. The Row position refills at once and acquire boxes resolve immediately [Main pp. 13, 20]; a place-Spy box pushes the shared `acquisition_spy` frame and a Contract box opens the Contract market, both only after the Intrigue card has finished resolving. | With no target within the cap the slot offers only `skip_intrigue_acquisition` (OQ-057 (6), codec v100). From 2026-10-06 to codec v153 that was reached only from Impress, whose swords keep it playable: an option whose only effect is the acquisition (Inspire Awe) was not offered without a target, judged on the slot's own two lists (`acquisition.acquirable_reserve_card_ids`, `acquirable_imperium_instance_ids`; OQ-057 (6) re-ruling, codec v138). Since codec v154 Inspire Awe has no cost and is played without a target too, its slot offering only `skip_intrigue_acquisition` (OQ-113 (C), user ruling 2026-10-24). Acquire-box follow-up frames therefore sit above the timing frame while the Intrigue card is already in `intrigue_discard`; the consecutive-pass reset happens before they open. Contract completion for the acquired card is checked, though no card within cost 3 carries one. |
| Influence at the top | A `GainInfluence` slot never offers a Faction whose cube is already at 6, where the gain is lost (`effect_interpreter.influence_gain_candidates`, `influence.influence_can_rise`; OQ-060 extended 2026-10-06). A Faction the same line just lowered with `LoseInfluence` can always be regained (Change Allegiances). A line with a cost whose rewards are all such Influence is not offered (OQ-071; `effect_interpreter._choice_reward_block` until 2026-10-06, now `reward_can_change_something`): Imperium Politics with Emperor and Spacing Guild at 6, Buy Access with all four (cost lines, unchanged by OQ-113 (C)); with one Faction still below the top Buy Access is offered and pays that one. From 2026-10-06 to codec v153 a cost-free option whose only effect is such a gain was not offered either (Rapid Engineering's three-Tech option with all four at 6; OQ-071 card extension 2); since v154 it is played, its dead slots offering only `resolve_intrigue_influence_without_faction`, and the play is noted as `intrigue_without_effect` (OQ-113 (C), user ruling 2026-10-24). When another effect of the option is live (Disguised Bureaucrat's spice) or one Faction of a "Choose two" can still rise, the card is played and the dead slot offers only `resolve_intrigue_influence_without_faction`, which also pays a "Choose two" Faction already named (one template in every catalog, codec v137). | User decision 2026-09-29 (OQ-071): "비용만 내고 보상을 받지 못하는 경우는 없어야 한다". The page greys a withheld cost line with "Every Faction it can raise is already at the top (6)". 보조 증거(공식 규칙 아님): the Steam app never offers a Faction at 6 here either (로컬 분석, 2026-10-06). Tests: `tests/unit/rules/test_intrigue.py` (`test_imperium_politics_never_offers_a_faction_at_the_top`, `test_buy_access_pays_the_one_faction_below_the_top_and_loses_the_other`, `test_change_allegiances_regains_the_faction_it_lost_at_the_top`), `tests/unit/rules/test_immortality_intrigue.py::test_disguised_bureaucrat_confirms_an_influence_no_cube_can_take`. |
| Mandatory costs | Every applicable cost of an ordinary option (one printed line, or one side of a `—OR—` card) is paid when the card is played; such an option whose applicable costs exceed the player's resources is not offered. A card printing separate lines without `—OR—` (`IntrigueOption.separate`: Change Allegiances, Strategic Stockpiling, Depart for Arrakis, Find Weakness, Questionable Methods) opens an `intrigue_effects` frame instead: cost-free lines resolve at play (`section_is_usable`; since codec v154 a dead one fizzles and still counts as used, OQ-113 (C); from 2026-10-06 to v153 it was neither resolved nor counted as used), each arrow line is its own `use_intrigue_effect(section)` paid when used, and `finish_intrigue_effects` is offered only once a line was used (`intrigue_effects_finish_is_open`). | Strict reading of FAQ p. 2 for ordinary options. Separate lines follow OQ-058 (user rulings 2026-09-09 and 2026-10-04 "최소 한 줄은 써야 함"): playable when one line is usable now (its condition holds and its cost is payable; a line with a cost also needs one reward that can change something, 2026-10-06, OQ-113 (C)), at least one line used before finishing (a cost-free line resolved at play counts, even when it fizzled, since codec v154), the rest optional. This replaced OQ-015 (b)'s reading that a second cost line gated by Influence becomes mandatory once the gate is met. |
| Discard order | The card stays in the owner's Intrigue hand while it resolves (including while an `intrigue_choice` frame is open) and reaches `intrigue_discard` after its effects resolve. | Card conservation holds at every state. A draw the card causes can never reshuffle the card itself. Cards discarded while a reshuffle is pending stay in the discard. |
| Deck exhaustion | `rules/intrigue_deck.py` draws what the deck holds, then shuffles the discard through a replayable chance frame for the remainder [FAQ p. 2]. With both piles empty the draw stops short and logs the public event `intrigue_draw_short` (counts only), which the play server's dry run turns into a warning on the step before the click (user ruling 2026-10-02, L2-Q4: "로그 + 클릭 전 경고"); a draw that needs a reshuffle logs it when the shuffle is asked for, since both pile sizes are public. Every other draw site (board effects, Combat rewards, acquisition and trash triggers, Agent-card and Reveal draws, the Bene Gesserit Influence bonus) draws what it can and queues the shortfall in `GameState.pending_intrigue_draws`; the dispatcher resolves the queue before the next player decision. | `intrigue_trash` is never reshuffled [Main p. 20]. An Intrigue card's own draw resolves before the card is discarded, so the card never joins the reshuffle; queued draws from other effects resolve after their transition, so a card discarded by that transition may be reshuffled. The Influence track-bonus event now reports only the count, not the card identity [Main p. 7]. |
| Agent-turn recruits | Troops recruited by a Plot during the owner's turn are recorded on the `turn` or `agent_effects` frame; the effect frame inherits the pre-placement count, so they may be deployed if the deployment group is still pending. | Once deployment for the turn has been resolved, later recruits stay in the garrison. |
| Units mid-Reveal | Troops or sandworms that a Plot puts into the Conflict during the owner's Reveal turn update `combat_strength` and the Reveal frame's strength like Desert Power's sandworm: the first units also make the revealed sword strength count [Main p. 13]. Before the Reveal they simply move. | A section whose only reward is the detonation icon still applies once the Shield Wall is gone (since codec v154, OQ-113 (C)): the icon is no choice then and the line fizzles, so Detonation's icon option is played all the same; before, it was not applicable and Detonation offered only its deployment option. |
| Harvest accounting | Spice paid for an Intrigue is added to the seat's `spice_spent_turn`, like every other Spice payment of the turn; Spice an Intrigue grants counts like any other gain, including a Plot played before the Agent placement (2026-10-06). | Harvest Spice Contracts count Spice gained from every source during the turn at a Maker space [Main p. 16], so paying Spice must not hide the harvest while Intrigue gains legitimately contribute. The Agent-turn frame keys `spice_at_placement` and `spice_spent_after_placement` were removed on 2026-10-06: after the whole-turn change they were written but never read. |
| Visibility | `intrigue_played` and `intrigue_cost_paid` events are public, and so is `intrigue_without_effect` (a play that changes nothing, OQ-113 (C)); draws report only counts. | Card identity stays hidden until played [Main p. 7]. |
| Choice slots | `choose_intrigue_faction(faction[, alliance_recipient])` resolves a `LoseInfluence` or multi-Faction `GainInfluence` step; `choose_intrigue_discard(card_id)` resolves a `DiscardFromHand` step; `detonate_shield_wall`/`keep_shield_wall` resolve a `DestroyShieldWall` step; `deploy_intrigue_troops(count)` resolves a `DeployFromGarrison` step; `trash_intrigue_card(card_id)`/`decline_intrigue_trash` resolve a `TrashPersonalCard` step; `place_intrigue_spy(post_id)`, `recall_spy_for_intrigue(post_id)`, and `decline_intrigue_spy` resolve `PlaceSpy` and `RecallSpy` steps — a `PlaceSpy` slot must place the Spy while one is in the supply and a post can take it (OQ-057 (14)); `decline_intrigue_spy` is offered only when the supply is empty (the preparatory recall stays optional) or no post can take it, and the slot offers at most one preparatory recall, only one that can lead to the placement (any own Spy while an unoccupied target post exists, otherwise only a Spy that is the sole occupant of an allowed post) `[Main pp. 11, 20]`. Once every cost slot is paid, `resolve_intrigue_rewards` lets the owner resolve the option's automatic rewards before, between, or after the remaining reward slots (OQ-015 (d), user ruling 2026-09-02, codec v83), so Cunning may draw first and trash the drawn card; before that ruling choice slots always resolved before the automatic rewards. | Losing Influence reuses the shared transition, so Friendship VP loss and Alliance return/transfer (with a recipient choice on ties) behave exactly as in the Reveal exchange. Opponents have no legal actions while the frame is open. |
