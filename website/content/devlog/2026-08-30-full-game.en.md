## From a round to a game

Personal cards, Intrigue, leader abilities, and endgame processing were filled in, with the CHOAM Module connected too. Repeated effects used shared parts, while decision frames represented pauses for choices.

A full-game random runner was added and the PettingZoo interface moved from a single round to a complete game. AI input contained the seat's permitted observations and a legal-action mask.

## Rewards and verification

At this stage the environment used terminal win/loss rewards rather than intermediate VP changes. This was a baseline for later experiments, not a description of every current training setting.

Twelve base-game and six CHOAM games completed through the environment, with zero-sum terminal rewards checked. Random play also exposed a Spy-placement failure when a recalled Spy had already been consumed by another effect. Full-game checks began complementing individual card tests.
