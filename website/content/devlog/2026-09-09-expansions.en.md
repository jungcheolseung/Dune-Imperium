## Optional expansions

Bloodlines and Tech Module became selectable configurations. Commanders, Skills, cards, conflicts, and contracts were connected, together with interface displays and heuristic values. Save documents retained the selected options.

This supported expansions/modules combined with Uprising, not the complete Rise of Ix game.

## Adding Immortality

Research and Tleilaxu tracks, the separate card row, Graft, and card effects were implemented. Expansion decisions entered observations and the action catalog. Bloodlines contract tokens and follow-up fixes continued through September 9.

## Combination checks

Random games with expansions exposed double reshuffles, card-specific deadlocks, and selection cycles. Regression tests and combination runs checked the fixes. Enabling an expansion and teaching a model its new choices remained separate tasks.
