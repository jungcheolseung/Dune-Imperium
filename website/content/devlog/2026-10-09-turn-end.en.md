## The third report

The engine used to close an Agent turn by itself the moment the last effect resolved. This had been raised twice in September (the screen-only fix of September 23 is in the [earlier entry]({{< relref path="devlog/2026-09-27-ui-korean.md" lang="en" >}})) and was stated a third time on September 30: even when no action is left, a turn ends only when the player presses End turn. Before ending, the player may still use Family Atomics to swap the Imperium Row, return a specimen to the supply, or play another Intrigue card.

The September fix changed only the server and the screen. The server held the last step and handed it to the "End turn ▶" button, but inside the engine the next seat's turn was already open, so the seat that was waiting had no legal action. That blocked a Plot Intrigue, Family Atomics, a specimen return, a Tech tile flip, and a Sardaukar Commander recruit after the last effect. In the survey at the time, 11 of 70 automatic closes had been preceded by one of these being legal. The official documents have no end-of-turn step for an Agent turn (`[Main pp. 8-11]`), and a Plot Intrigue can be played at any point during one of your own Agent or Reveal turns (`[Main p. 8]`). The automatic close was an engine default, not a rule.

## The engine stops closing turns (October 1–2)

Every Agent turn now closes only through the player's End turn (OQ-095, codec v125).

- The turn stays open after the last effect, so a Plot, Family Atomics, a specimen return, a Tech flip, and a Commander recruit are all still possible. The engine has exactly one place that closes a turn.
- Follow-ups left by the last effect (a Skill, a Navigation play, a Contract, an Emperor-track Spy, research, a Subcommittee row) are resolved inside the open turn instead of after it closed, and count as that turn's.
- The press fizzles stalled Agent boxes and held Contract icons and trashes a Usurp card. If that produces a mandatory Contract or new troops to deploy, the turn reopens.
- Cards that pass the turn (Withdrawn, Litany Against Fear) end the turn by being played.
- The `turn_closed` machinery built for the old automatic close (retroactive marking, queue fields, 18 probes) was removed. Across 7 configurations and 112 games, the legal actions offered, the actions chosen, and the events matched byte for byte before and after.

Places that used to skip silently became decision windows. The ruling was that anything that moved on automatically without a window should open one. Desert Power and Imperial Privilege open a confirmation even with no target. Holy War handles all losses first and then all Spy moves, in the printed order. A Commander recruit without a Skill shows a confirmation, a CHOAM Contract icon that cannot be taken is held and then fizzled, and a draw that comes up short because the deck and discard are empty shows a warning badge before the click. This bundle merged the same day.

The merge was checked with 2,985 pytest tests, 31 browser E2E scripts, and a random soak of 350 games plus a 144-game census (no failures). An independent review found five minor defects, which were fixed. The change also affects the AIs. Every Agent turn gained one end decision, and the heuristic AI now flips Tech tiles and recruits Commanders at the end of non-combat turns in Bloodlines and Tech games, so earlier A/B and tournament numbers cannot be compared with later ones. The play network of the time often chose a specimen return in the end window (79 of 293 times in the old deployment window). Retraining that is covered in [another entry]({{< relref path="devlog/2026-10-06-app-search-ai.md" lang="en" >}}).

## A defect found after the merge (October 2)

An independent review found a defect that predated the change. When a combat Intrigue card left a window on top of the cycle (Reach Agreement's Contract market, Battlefield Research's Tech window), the count of consecutive passes was kept. After the seats before it had passed, the seat that played the card could pass once more and the Conflict resolved without the remaining seats answering. The rule ends the window only when all combat participants pass in a row (`[Main p. 14]`). The cycle below the window is now recounted when the card finishes, and when the cycle empties because every participant has dropped out, a "Combat Intrigue ended" event is logged (codec v127).

## The press becomes final (October 8–9)

### Follow-ups left by the press

During a training evaluation, a search seat's playout raised an error. Long Reach pays the first faction at End turn when the second cannot be chosen, and if that payment touches an influence-track bonus it can create a follow-up such as the Emperor 4 Spy. Unless a Usurp card was involved, the press closed the turn without looking at that follow-up. The state arises from public information alone, so it could happen in a real game. A first fix listed the kinds of follow-up, and a review found a kind that was missing. The list was dropped and every End turn press now goes down one "finishing" path. Follow-ups resolve above it, and the engine closes or reopens the turn at the end of the transition.

That the result stayed the same was checked against the old engine. On 200 games (120 random, 40 heuristic, 40 mixed with a rule-based AI ported from the computer opponent of the official digital edition (Steam), all expansions and Immortality), every step's state hash and event kinds were recorded, and replaying the same moves on the new engine matched on all 190,107 steps (15,487 presses). Three rare cases that do differ were found by an independent review; all move toward the OQ-095 ruling and are fixed by regression tests.

### An End turn button when it was not the player's turn

An oddity showed up in a saved game: an "End turn" button appeared when it was not that player's turn to end one, after the Round 1 combat, at the start of Arrakeen Scouts, and at its end. The cause was the order in the server. After a human seat's out-of-turn unit (combat rewards, a Scouts row, a Control defense) ended, the server waited for a press, but first resolved the chance that followed (the next round's shuffle and draw, the next Scouts card). By the time the button appeared, the undo window had already closed. A player may want to move the two Spies from a reward before confirming, and instead only the End turn button was left.

The server now holds before that chance and lets the press resolve it. Whether the unit is over is judged only from whether decisions of the unit remain on the stack, so the fact of holding does not reveal the card to be drawn. A unit end with nothing to undo is not held. In the browser, choices that cannot be undone once sent outside a turn (for example, a reward that leads straight into the next round's reveal) are only selected, and the "Confirm ▶" button sends them. Before sending, the player can pick something else or cancel with Esc. The button is not called End turn because whether the unit continues after that step can depend on the card about to be revealed, which cannot be known in advance.

### The engine pauses before a reveal

That left two problems. The same reward choice appeared as "End turn ▶" with undo when the next round needed a shuffle and as "Confirm ▶" when it did not, so players saw two shapes. Worse, the difference depended on whether another seat held Harvest Cells, which exposed a hidden hand. An investigation found this and it was reproduced (Immortality, round 8).

The solution chosen is for the engine to pause on a chance decision with a single outcome before a reveal (OQ-103, codec v140). The places are fixed by public facts only: before payout to each recipient after the first, after all rewards and before the Conflict-end window, before every Round Start shuffle and reveal after the first round, and after a Control defense before the draw. The pause's answer uses no random numbers, so seeded games and AI decisions do not change. Server code stayed as it was, and reward choices, the Conflict-end window, and Control defense are all held in front of this pause and can be undone. "Confirm ▶" remains only where the choice itself causes a reveal (an Imperium Row card refill, a Scouts row that draws cards, and so on).

Two alternatives were rejected. A new "continue" action would make every seat answer a forced choice each round, and the heuristic and random AIs use a random number even for a single option, so every seeded game would change. Changing only the screen widened the hidden-information leak in simple games and added clicks in games without one.

The independent review confirmed several defects. The pause had moved the completion of Earn Any Alliance earlier and changed the result against the old engine (3 of 344 games), the last round had no Round Start so a Harvest Cells leak remained, and the action log hid events carried by the pause step. All were fixed and then checked against the old engine again: on 200 random games, 120 draft games, and 24 heuristic games, the state at every player decision, the actions, the final state, the event log, and the Victory Points matched. v139 saves no longer open; there was no plan to use saved games, so it went ahead.

### The Conflict-end window as a public fact (v141)

After the pause, one problem remained: the Conflict-end window opened only for a seat holding a usable Harvest Cells, so whether the window opened exposed a hand. By decision the window now opens from public facts (Immortality in use, at least 3 units in the Conflict, at least one Intrigue card, and not both Harvest Cells copies in public zones), and offers only "Don't use" when nothing can be played. When there is a single human seat, the server answers a window offering only "Don't use" on its behalf; with several humans it does not, because the waiting itself would be a clue. Earn Any Alliance now completes at the end of the step in which the Alliance is taken. In combat, troops return only after all rewards are given (`[Main p. 14]`), so completion always comes before that. This bundle is codec v141.

## What was checked, and what remains

Through v141 the checks were 6,726 pytest tests, Ruff and mypy, and 53 browser E2E scripts. By then an End turn press followed one path, and an irreversible out-of-turn choice was either "Confirm ▶" or "End turn ▶" with undo. The later cases where optional effects lapse at End turn, and the Reveal-choice cleanup, are outside this entry.

The checks centred on local browser E2E and the random comparisons used in review.
