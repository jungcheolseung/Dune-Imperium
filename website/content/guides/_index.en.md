Each game has four seats. With one human, fill three seats with AI. With friends, adjust the human seats accordingly.

The host installs and runs the game; invited guests use their browsers. Images are optional for the text interface.

Before installing, check [requirements]({{< relref path="downloads/_index.md" lang="en" >}}). For problems, see [help]({{< relref path="help/_index.md" lang="en" >}}).
